Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Request Call Back
What internship programs does 3Skill offer? −

3Skill offers internship programs across multiple domains, including Web Development, Data Analytics, AI & ML, and more. You can choose a 2-Month or 3-Month Internship Program depending on how deep you want to go.

We also offer a flexible Project-Based Internship that can run anywhere from 1 to 6 months, where you work on a single real project in your chosen domain, start to finish.

How many students have completed an internship with 3Skill? +

Over 50,000 students have already learned with 3Skill and completed internships across our different programs.

Can I pick my own domain for the internship? +

Yes. Our Project-Based Internship lets you choose the domain you want to work in, so you can focus on the skill area most relevant to your goals.

What is 3Skill's refund policy? −

As per our terms and conditions, you're eligible for a 100% money refund.

To be eligible, you're required to complete the course or internship you enrolled in, as outlined in our terms and conditions.

How do I request a refund? +

You can reach out to our support team through the Help section, and they'll guide you through the refund process as per our policy.

Are the classes live or pre-recorded? −

All classes at 3Skill are conducted live by real mentors. If you're ever unable to attend a live class, you'll still get access to the full recording, so you never miss out.

How many students have taken 3Skill's courses? +

3Skill has already trained over 50,000 students across its courses and programs.

What if I have doubts during a live class? +

You can ask questions directly during the live session, and mentors are available to help you understand every concept clearly.

Do I get a certificate after completing my course or internship? −

Yes. Every course and internship at 3Skill comes with a verified certificate on completion.

Is the certificate recognized? +

Our certificates are recognized by 99% of colleges across India, as well as by companies hiring across India.

Can I use the certificate for job applications? +

Yes, the certificate is verifiable and can be added to your resume, LinkedIn profile, or used directly in job applications.
