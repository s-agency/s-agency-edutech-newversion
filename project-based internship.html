All you need to know about our internship, projects, benefits & certification.

It's a project-based internship across 15 domains. You pick a domain, choose a duration from 1 to 6 months, and work on submitting a real-world project — with live doubt-clearing sessions available whenever you need help.

Any student from any college or university can join — 1st year to final year. Whether you're from CSE, IT, or any other branch, as long as you're eager to learn and build, you're welcome!

We currently offer 15 domains — including Web Development, AI & Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Cyber Security, UI/UX, Digital Marketing, Business Analytics and more. Pick the one that matches your interest.

Choose your domain and duration (1 to 6 months), pick an upcoming batch date, and work on your real-world project. Live doubt-clearing sessions are available throughout so you're never stuck.

Top performers are considered for a paid internship opportunity at 3Skill, based on their project quality and consistency.
